# Curated journal lists

This directory contains smaller curated journal sets used as optional matching
sources in WOS Aide.

## Files and official sources

### `AJG2024.csv`

Academic Journal Guide 2024 (Chartered Association of Business Schools).

- Official AJG 2024 information: https://charteredabs.org/academic-journal-guide/academic-journal-guide-2024
- AJG 2024 methodology PDF: https://assets.charteredabs.org/ajg-2024-methodology.pdf

### `UTD24.csv`

The 24 journals used by the UT Dallas Top 100 Business School Research
Rankings.

- Official ranking/journal-list page:
  https://jindal.utdallas.edu/the-utd-top-100-business-school-research-rankings/

### `FT50.csv`

Financial Times business-school research journal list.

- Financial Times 2026 source:
  https://www.ft.com/content/db863a0a-6524-45f9-bc52-fee997634bdc

These sources are maintained by their respective organizations. Refresh the
local CSV snapshots from the official pages when a new edition is released.
